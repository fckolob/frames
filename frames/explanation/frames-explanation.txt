Frames is a web app designed to help companies that craft aluminum openings, especially in Uruguay. The app lets users enter multiple openings in different types and colours. The app then calculates the length of every necessary piece and displays the glass size. The app also calculates how many bars are needed to craft these openings and shows details for each type and colour. The app gives the user a cutting plan to cut the bars in an efficient way to avoid waste. The app allows the user to export the cutting plan, the production report, and the materials report as PDFs or print them.
